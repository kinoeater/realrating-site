How RealScore works

01

Open any business on Maps

RealScore runs automatically in the background. No setup, no clicking — just browse Google Maps as usual.

02

Detection in under a second

If a defamation removal notice exists, RealScore finds it — even when Google buries it in the Reviews tab where most users never look.

03

See the real picture

A badge shows how many reviews were removed and estimates the rating those reviews would produce if counted — calculated using the same method as Pieter Levels' Real Rating Calculator.

Detected in three languages

Google Maps shows the removal notice in the UI language of the user — not always German. RealScore detects the notice regardless of which language Maps is set to.

All number formats are handled: "11 to 20", "Two to five", "Zwei bis fünf", "Over 250", "Eine Bewertung".
